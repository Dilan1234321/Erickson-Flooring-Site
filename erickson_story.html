"Modern mastery. Old-world soul."

Erickson Flooring brings architectural precision to the world's most versatile flooring material.
This is the Chicago Standard, reimagined for Tampa.

I grew up in Chicago, a city built on steel and sweat. I spent my twenties on my knees in hundred-year-old brownstones, breathing in dust that was older than my grandfather. It was there I learned that a floor isn't just a surface — it's the foundation of a home's character.

Most installers see vinyl as a "shortcut." I see it as an engineering challenge. Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) is the most resilient, high-performance material in modern construction, but it only lives up to its name when it's installed with surgical precision.

"We don't just lay planks. We lay the foundation for your life's memories."

When I moved to Tampa, I saw a market flooded with "fast" work. Beautiful homes were being fitted with premium vinyl that squeaked after six months because the subfloor wasn't leveled. Seams were separating because humidity wasn't accounted for.

I started Erickson Flooring to change that. I brought the Chicago Standard to Florida — the refusal to compromise, the obsession with detail, and the understanding that the subfloor is the only part that matters.

I specialize exclusively in Luxury Vinyl. Why? Because mastery requires focus. By dedicating myself to one craft, I've perfected the techniques required to make LVP look and feel indistinguishable from solid wood, with a durability that outlasts them all.
