The Craftsman

Behind the Grain

A journey from the historic renovations of the Windy City to the luxury estates of Tampa Bay.

Forged in Chicago.
Perfected in Tampa.

There is no better classroom for a flooring craftsman than Chicago. Working on 100-year-old historic brownstones teaches you respect for the material. You learn that wood lives, breathes, and moves. You learn that a floor isn't just a surface—it's structural art.

I spent my formative years restoring floors that had survived a century of brutal winters and humid summers. That environment doesn't forgive shortcuts. If you don't acclimate the wood properly, it warps. If you don't sand with precision, the old stain bleeds through.

When I moved to Tampa, I brought that "Chicago Standard" with me. In a market often rushed by new construction timelines, Erickson Flooring stands apart by slowing down, obsessing over the subfloor, and delivering a product that will outlast the house itself.

The Art of the Sand

Refinishing is where the true master is revealed. It requires a delicate touch to remove imperfections without gouging the wood.

  • Dustless Systems: We use hospital-grade HEPA vacuums. No dust clouds.
  • Water Popping: A technique to open the grain for deeper, richer color penetration.
  • Commercial Grade Sealants: We use Bona Traffic HD, the industry leader for durability.

Subfloor Obsession

A beautiful floor on a bad subfloor is a ticking time bomb. Squeaks, gaps, and cupping happen beneath the surface.

Before a single plank is laid, we laser-level the concrete or plywood. We grind down high spots and fill low spots. If the foundation isn't perfect, we don't start. That is the Erickson promise.
