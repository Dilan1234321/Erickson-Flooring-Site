Our Philosophy

Luxury vinyl is the most versatile material in modern construction, but only when installed by hands that refuse to compromise.

Most installers treat LVP as a quick job. We treat it as an engineering discipline. The difference between a floor that impresses on day one and a floor that still impresses on year ten comes down to three things: subfloor mastery, precision cutting, and humidity science.

Foundation Work
01
The Invisible Craft

Master the Subfloor

A floor is only as good as what's beneath it. While most installers spend 20% of their time on preparation, we spend nearly 50%. This is the invisible work that separates a floor that lasts from a floor that fails.

We grind down high spots in concrete, fill low spots with precision-leveling compound, and ensure plywood subfloors are screwed tight with zero flex. Every square foot is tested with a straightedge and moisture meter before a single plank is placed.

The Erickson Standard: No subfloor variance greater than 3/16" over 10 feet. That's twice as strict as the industry standard — and the reason our floors never squeak.
Millimeter Accuracy
02
The Signature

The Precision Cut

We don't believe in "close enough." Every corner, every transition strip, and every vent cutout is measured twice and cut with millimeter accuracy. The goal is a finish so seamless that you can't tell where one plank ends and the next begins.

Where standard practice allows a 1/4" gap hidden by molding, we achieve joints tight enough that no filler, caulk, or trim is needed to disguise our work. The cuts speak for themselves.

Why it matters: Imprecise cuts create gaps that collect dust, allow moisture infiltration, and cause planks to shift over time. Precision isn't cosmetic — it's structural.
"The floor should feel like it was always meant to be there — like the house was built around it."
Reid Erickson — Founder
Vertical Craft
03
The Ultimate Test

Staircase Transformations

Staircases are the true test of an installer's skill. Every tread nose, every riser, every stringer requires custom templating and precision wrapping. There's no room for error — every cut is visible from multiple angles.

We template each step individually because no two stairs are identical. Our bullnose wrapping technique creates a monolithic look that rivals solid hardwood, with the durability of rigid-core vinyl and none of the creaking.

The difference: Most installers use pre-made stair nosing that never quite matches. We custom-cut every nose from matching plank material for a seamless visual flow.
Climate Mastery
04
The Florida Factor

Humidity Science

Florida's subtropical climate is the silent killer of flooring installations. The gap between air conditioning and outdoor humidity creates a constant battle of expansion and contraction that most installers never account for.

We acclimate every plank to your home's specific microclimate. We test concrete moisture with calcium chloride and relative humidity probes. And we calibrate expansion gaps to Tampa's seasonal humidity range — not some generic spec from a manufacturer in the Midwest.

The Chicago-to-Tampa advantage: Learning to handle Chicago's brutal freeze-thaw cycles taught us respect for what temperature and moisture can do. Florida's humidity is a different beast — and we've mastered both.
