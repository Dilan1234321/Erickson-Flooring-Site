"The floor remembers."

It remembers the weight of your furniture, the scuff of your boots,
and the first steps of your children.

I grew up in Chicago, where buildings weren't just built; they were forged. I spent my twenties on my knees in hundred-year-old brownstones on the Gold Coast, breathing in dust that was older than my grandfather.

Those winters were brutal. The cold would crack steel if you let it. It taught me that materials matter. Wood expands, tile cracks, carpet fades. I spent years searching for a floor that could truly handle life without sacrificing beauty.

My mentor was an old-world craftsman who never owned a laser level in his life but could spot a 1/16th inch dip from across the room. He taught me that the subfloor is everything. With modern luxury vinyl, this is the golden rule. If the foundation isn't perfect, the locking mechanism fails.

"We don't just lay planks. We engineer a waterproof foundation for your life."

When I moved to Tampa, I saw the damage humidity does to traditional hardwood. I saw "waterproof" floors failing because installers didn't level the concrete first. I saw cheap, plastic-looking vinyl sold as "luxury."

I started Erickson Flooring to specialize exclusively in High-End Luxury Vinyl (LVP/LVT). To bring architectural precision to a material that is often treated as a DIY project. We treat vinyl with the same respect as a $20,000 hardwood floor.

I don't have a massive crew of strangers. When you hire Erickson, you get me. You get the guy who stays late to hand-scrape the corners. You get the guy who loses sleep over a single gap. You get a floor that will outlive us both.

Honor the Layout

Vinyl has a grain, a repeat pattern, and a flow. We don't just snap pieces together. We calculate the layout to ensure no sliver-cuts at the walls, randomizing the pattern so it looks indistinguishable from real timber.

Subfloor Obsession

Luxury vinyl telegraphs every imperfection. We spend 40% of our time grinding concrete and applying self-leveling compound. The result is a floor that feels solid, sounds quiet, and never bounces.
